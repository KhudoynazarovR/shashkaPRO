1. Umumiy

ShashkaPRO — rus shashkasi o‘yini: Telegram mini-ilovasi (@DraughtsPRObot), sayt (shashkapro.onrender.com) va Android ilovasi (uz.shashkapro.app). Ushbu siyosat qaysi ma'lumotlarni yig‘ishimiz, ulardan qanday foydalanishimiz va sizning huquqlaringizni tushuntiradi.

2. Qanday ma'lumotlar yig‘iladi

  • Telegram hisobi ma'lumotlari: Telegram ID raqamingiz, ismingiz, familiyangiz, foydalanuvchi nomingiz (username), til va profil rasmingiz. Ular Telegram orqali kirganingizda Telegram tomonidan beriladi.
  • Google hisobi ma'lumotlari (Google orqali kirsangiz): ismingiz, familiyangiz va Google hisobingizning ichki identifikatori. Email manzilingiz va parolingiz saqlanmaydi.
  • O‘yin ma'lumotlari: o‘yinlar natijalari, reyting (ELO), o‘yin ichidagi tangalar, yechilgan masalalar, turnir va liga natijalari, yutuqlar, do‘stlar ro‘yxati, oxirgi faollik vaqti.
  • Xabarlar: o‘yin xonalari va turnir chatlarida yozgan xabarlaringiz.
  • Qurilmada saqlanadigan sozlamalar: ovoz, fon va kirish sessiyasi qurilmangiz xotirasida (localStorage) saqlanadi.
  • Reklama (faqat Android ilovada): Google AdMob reklama identifikatori (Advertising ID), qurilma turi, IP manzil va reklama bilan o‘zaro ta'sir ma'lumotlarini to‘playdi.

Biz telefon raqamingiz, kontaktlaringiz, joylashuvingiz, rasmlaringiz yoki fayllaringizga kirmaymiz.

3. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

  • Hisobingizni yaratish va kirishni ta'minlash;
  • onlayn o‘yinlar, reytinglar, turnirlar, ligalar va chatni ishlatish;

4. Uchinchi tomonlar

  • Google Sign-In — Google orqali kirish.
  • Telegram — kirish, bot xabarlari va Telegram Stars orqali to‘lovlar (faqat Telegram ichida). telegram.org/privacy

Shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz. Ismingiz, reytingingiz va natijalaringiz boshqa o‘yinchilarga reyting jadvallari va turnirlarda ko‘rinadi.

5. Saqlash muddati va xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar hisobingiz faol ekan saqlanadi. Ulanish HTTPS orqali shifrlanadi, kirish Telegram imzosi bilan tekshiriladi.

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Developer: Xudoynazarov Ramazon (ShashkaPRO)

ShashkaPRO is a Russian draughts game available as a Telegram Mini App (@DraughtsPRObot), a website (shashkapro.onrender.com) and an Android app (uz.shashkapro.app).

Information we collect

  • Telegram account data provided by Telegram when you sign in: Telegram user ID, first and last name, username, language and profile photo.
  • Google account data (if you sign in with Google): first and last name and your Google account's internal identifier. We do not store your email address or password.
  • Game data: game results, rating (ELO), in-game coins, solved puzzles, tournament and league results, achievements, friends list and last activity time.
  • Messages you send in game rooms and tournament chats.
  • On-device settings (sound, background, sign-in session) stored in your device's local storage.
  • Advertising (Android app only): Google AdMob collects the advertising ID, device information, IP address and ad interaction data.

We do not access your phone number, contacts, location, photos or files.

How we use it

To create and sign in to your account; to run online games, ratings, tournaments, leagues and chat; to calculate achievements and rewards; to improve the service and prevent abuse; and to show ads in the Android app.

Third parties

Google Sign-In (sign-in), Telegram (sign-in, bot messages, Telegram Stars payments inside Telegram only), Google AdMob (ads in the Android app) and Render (hosting). We do not sell personal data. Your name, rating and results are visible to other players in leaderboards and tournaments.

Retention and security

Data is kept while your account is active. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS and sign-in is verified with Telegram signatures.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time on the account deletion page. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
